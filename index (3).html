Dialogflow chatbot demo

StarGuide

A small chatbot that helps you explore the night sky. Ask it about a constellation and it will get ready to tell you more. Open the chat in the bottom right corner.

Try asking

  • Hi
  • Tell me about Canes Venatici
  • Is Cassiopeia a constellation?
  • Why is it called the Big Dipper?
